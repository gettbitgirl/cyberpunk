#### Get with the program

It's been **four years** since [[Saburo Arasaka]], the former CEO of [[Arasaka|The Arasaka Corporation]] was assassinated by [[Goro Takemura]]. In his stead, his son [[Yorinobu Arasaka]] took his place. **Yorinobu's** leadership destabilized [[Arasaka]], greatly weakening it's political influence worldwide and in **Night City**. That's why, as of two years ago, **Yorinobu** was ousted from his role as CEO of [[Arasaka]]. Word on the street is he ran off with some **nomads**. Nobody's seen the man since.

These days, [[Arasaka]] is run by **Saburo's** granddaughter [[Michiko Arasaka]]. **Michiko** was brought on in the hopes that a new direction for [[Arasaka]] would stabilize the company after the chaos that **Yorinobu** caused. Her leadership has caused quite a stir, enacting **sweeping internal reforms** and **mass layoffs**, as well as a public campaign stating that she's cracking down on corruption from higher ups.

[[Militech]] and [[Kang-Tao]] have taken the opportunity to fill the power vacuum left by [[Arasaka]], hiring many of the engineers looking for work after being **unceremoniously sacked** by their previous employer.  Here in [[Night City]], **'Saka** still reigns supreme, in large part due to the newly elected mayor [[Amanda Kimbal]], who just so happens to be a close personal friend of [[Michiko Arasaka]]... In her time in office **Kimbal** has furthered the initiative of former mayor [[Jefferson Peralez]] to limit the special powers granted only to [[NCPD]], and instead accepting policing contracts from other corporate security forces besides [[NCPD]]. The majority of which have been awarded to [[Arasaka]] as of late.
